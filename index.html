Frequently Asked

Questions couples should ask early

How early should we enquire?

Popular dates and destinations can book far ahead. Enquire once the date or shortlist is reasonably clear.

Are travel and stay included?

They should be shown separately or clearly included in the written proposal based on city, crew and event schedule.

When are photographs and films delivered?

The contract should specify preview, gallery, film, revision and album timelines rather than using a vague verbal promise.
